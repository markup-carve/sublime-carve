Carve 0.1.10
============

Fixes
  - Block context follows the engine: a list marker or caption line
    after a paragraph is prose, front matter without a closing ---
    ends at the first non-YAML line, and an inline note ends with its
    paragraph.
  - Headings, list markers, definitions, tables and container fences
    are highlighted inside > quotes, and a container's closer ends a
    fence or other block left open inside it.
  - Bold and strikethrough need a word boundary (2*3*4 stays text),
    every ASCII punctuation character escapes, ==x== is no longer
    highlighted, and a | line needs its closing pipe to be a table row.
  - A line of many unclosed brackets no longer takes seconds to
    highlight.
