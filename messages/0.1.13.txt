Carve 0.1.13
============

Improvements
  - The syntax follows the Carve 0.1.14 readings: front matter without a
    closer runs to the end of the document, braced cell alignment
    (|={>}, |{<^}) is scoped as table alignment, a letter or roman dot
    marker needs two spaces, - - - is a thematic break, mention and tag
    names need an ASCII letter, and ->, <-, (c) and (r) are text.
  - Export as PDF from carve-lsp is documented, with the
    carve.pdf.crv2pdfPath and carve.pdf.browserPath settings.
