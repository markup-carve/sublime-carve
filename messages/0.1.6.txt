Carve 0.1.6
===========

Fixes
  - Include directives are highlighted by part: the target, each @option
    and its value, quoted or not. A quoted value may hold a }} pair, and
    the directive closes at the first pair outside a quoted run
    (markup-carve/carve#2013).
  - An unterminated {{ no longer opened an attribute block, which used to
    cost every line below it its inline highlighting.
  - A bare *, /, _, ~ or = inside a link destination, a link title or an
    autolink no longer opens or closes an emphasis run.
